PHYSICS DIAGNOSIS AND CHOSEN CHANGES
The old triangle began with one peg. First deflections could ride its expanding
outer edge; the measured distribution had strong edge concentration.
Default 12-row baseline: central-band share 26.10%, outer-four share 61.95%.
Central band means |pocketIndex-R/2| <= R/4; outer four means 0,1,R-1,R.
A larger-peg/lower-bounce candidate made edge concentration and trapping worse;
it was rejected. Candidate data is retained for diagnosis rather than hidden.

Chosen configuration (candidate-d, then rerun as after using authored assets):
Top row has 3 pegs; row r has r+3 pegs centered x=c-(r+2)/2.
For R rows: R*(R+5)/2 pegs, still R+1 pockets. Bottom-row pegs are the boundaries
between the R+1 pocket centers x=i-R/2. No reward-directed forces or steering.
Peg diameter .25; ball diameter .28; vertical row pitch .8; horizontal pitch 1.
Last row y=2; top row y=2+(R-1)*.8. Spawn 1 unit above top peg with x uniform
[-.22,.22], zero initial velocity. Gravity scale 1.6, friction .05, restitution
.6, linear damping .35, Continuous 2D collision detection and interpolation.
Outer wall centers +/-((R+1)/2+.1); bottom sensors/dividers retain alignment.
All values are authored in GameConfig.asset and shared across profiles.
Profiles change color and reward table only. No landing outcome is preselected.

MEASUREMENT METHOD AND RESULTS
PegRushDistribution.cs uses actual production board generation, ball prefab,
colliders, materials and Rigidbody2D solver in an isolated PhysicsScene2D.
Editor-only DistributionProbe observes trigger entry and uses the same lifecycle
thresholds. It resets position, rotation, velocity, angular velocity, damping,
age and stuck timer each sample. Batch size 50; ball-ball collisions disabled.
Fixed step .02s. Recorded seed = 741923 + rows. Continue sampling until 2,000
physical pocket completions, separately retaining every accepted/refunded drop.
Rows 8–16 each have 2,000 before and 2,000 after completions: 36,000 completed
comparison samples. Candidate experiments are additional independent runs.
This is Editor-engine measurement, not 36,000 WebGL UI activations.
Raw sample CSVs, per-row JSONs, Summary.csv/JSON and plots are in
SprintDoc/Polish/Distribution. JSON includes frequencies, plug-in standard errors
and 95% marginal Wilson intervals. Histograms show Wilson error bars.
Recorded durations are simulation seconds with one-step .02s quantization.
The before command explicitly restores baseline physics parameters for repeats.

After: 18,000 pocket completions, 18,001 accepted, one stuck refund (14 rows),
zero escaped balls, zero timeouts. At 12 rows: central-band 59.95%, outer-four
26.25%, mean 4.778s, p95 6.78s. Edge pockets remain reachable in every row test.
The shape is broadly center-weighted, not an exact binomial distribution.
Left/right z statistics and individual mirrored bins are reported with sampling
uncertainty. Finite counts are not perfectly symmetric; do not tune toward exact
matching samples or claim statistical certification. R12 left/right z=-2.43.
No casino-equivalent odds, guaranteed returns or fairness-certification claims.

REWARD REVIEW
Old Red 25x edges caused excessive virtual-wallet growth even after the physics
fix. On the new R12 samples, old tables averaged approximately 1.15/2.52/5.53
coins credited per coin reserved (Green/Gold/Red). This motivated an arcade
balancing change, separate from solver tuning. These are observed sample ratios,
not guaranteed future returns, casino odds or a payout certification.
New symmetric tables use e=abs(2*i/R-1), with integer hundredths:
 Green = round(75 + 75*e^2), range .75–1.5x.
 Gold  = round(50 + 250*e^3), range .5–3x.
 Red   = round(25 + 575*e^5), range .25–6x.
Odd row counts have two centers slightly above the formulas' minima.
Green is flatter; Gold intermediate; Red retains small center payouts and larger
edge rewards. See ArcadeCoinPacing.json for all 27 actual tables and observed
before/after table ratios on the same physical samples.
Fixed-point wallet and half-up rounding to .01 coin are unchanged.
Pending transactions still clone amount, rows, profile and complete reward table.
Older valid pending saves still refund once; save schema/key unchanged.

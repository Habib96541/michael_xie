# Fruit Ninja · Backyard Dojo

A Unity 6 fruit-slicing game with an illustrated orchard, crooked wooden signs, taped cardboard cards, and a warm garden-menu aesthetic.

## Play

- Open `Assets/Scenes/BackyardDojo.unity` and press **Play**.
- Or launch `Builds/Windows/Fruit Ninja.exe` after building. Keep its accompanying data folder and DLLs together.
- Hold the left mouse button and swipe, or drag a finger on a touchscreen. Click a mode card or swipe through its fruit to start.
- **Esc / P:** pause. **Space / Esc:** resume. **R:** retry from the results screen.

## Modes

- **Classic:** survive as long as possible. Three missed fruit or one sliced bomb ends the round.
- **Arcade:** 60 seconds, escalating waves, combo bonuses, rare critical slices, freeze and fruit-frenzy powerups. Bombs subtract 10 points.
- **Zen:** 90 seconds of fruit with no bombs and no miss penalties.

Slice at least three fruit in one stroke for a square-of-count bonus. High scores are stored separately for each mode. Lifetime fruit, best combos, sound preferences, and blade color persist locally with Unity PlayerPrefs.

## Project

Unity **6000.3.10f1**; uses the project's Universal Render Pipeline, Unity UI, and Input System packages. No additional packages or external services are needed to play. UI is generated from reusable textured paper meshes at runtime and scales with a centered 1600 × 900 stage.

- `Assets/FruitNinja/Scripts/FruitNinjaGame.cs`: modes, fruit trajectories, segment-based swipe detection, slicing, effects, sound, menus, and records.
- `Assets/FruitNinja/Scripts/PaperGraphic.cs`: textured cardboard, clipped fruit halves, blade trail, and button feedback.
- `Assets/FruitNinja/Editor/ProjectSetup.cs`: scene setup, Windows build command, and editor verification bridge.

Use **Backyard Dojo → Build Windows player** to rebuild. The editor's **Set up game scene** command recreates the dedicated scene.

## Validation

`Artifacts/smoke-result.txt` records gameplay checks, and `Artifacts/ui-result.txt` records actual Unity UI raycast and click-event checks. The verification routines are excluded from player builds. Screenshots and the build summary are in `Artifacts/`.

## Artwork and fonts

The two project artwork assets were generated with the built-in `image_gen` tool. Their prompts and saved paths are recorded in `Assets/FruitNinja/ArtGeneration.json`. Textured paper, interface meshes, particles, blade trails, and audio are generated by code.

Lilita One and Lato come from the Google Fonts repository. Their SIL Open Font License files are included beside the font assets.

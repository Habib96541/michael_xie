Sprint 04 proposal: Sharpen Flap Dash movement and pace
Status: tuning approved and implemented on 2026-11-02. The 17 Editor behavior checks pass; user play feel and understanding are pending. The user explicitly deferred building.

Why
The user's review says Flap Dash feels slow and lethargic and needs slightly sharper response and a faster pace.
Teach movement tuning: increase flap velocity and gravity together to shorten the hop while keeping its height nearly unchanged. Tune presentation timing separately from physics.

Starting point inspected
- Unity 6000.3.6f1.
- Flap speed 7, gravity scale 1.5, fall speed cap 12.
- Pipe speed 2.3, spawn interval 1.7 seconds, first spawn delay 1.3 seconds.
- Flap visual pulse 0.2 seconds, tilt smoothing rate 12.
- Input sampled in Update and physics applied in FixedUpdate, timestep 0.02 seconds.
- Player Rigidbody interpolation and continuous collision detection are already enabled.

Proposed first tuning pass
- Flap speed: 7 to 8.
- Gravity scale: 1.5 to 2.
- Pipe speed: 2.3 to 2.65 (about 15 percent faster).
- Spawn interval: 1.7 to 1.48 seconds, retaining nearly the same world-space pipe spacing.
- First spawn delay: 1.3 to 1.1 seconds.
- Flap visual pulse: 0.2 to 0.12 seconds.
- Tilt smoothing rate: 12 to 20.
- Keep physics timestep, collider size, gap size, input bindings, score and 60-second round duration.
- These are playtest candidates, not a measured latency fix. With ideal constant gravity, the new hop reaches its apex about 14 percent sooner at about 2 percent less height.
- The faster course changes challenge timing. Record it as a new prototype tuning version. The existing WebGL files remain the previous tuning; no build or archive changes are part of this sprint after the user's correction.

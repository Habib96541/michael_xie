Concepts to explain
- Awake caches required references once before play.
- Update reads frame input; FixedUpdate applies physics on the physics timestep.
- A simple enum records which round actions are currently allowed.
- A per-gate boolean prevents the same passage from awarding points twice.
- Inspector references keep dependencies visible without searching the scene each frame.
- Presentation displays state; RoundController owns the score and timer.

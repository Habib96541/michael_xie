Sprint 04: Sharpen Flap Dash movement and pace
Date: 2026-11-02
Status: implementation and Editor behavior checks complete; user play feel and understanding pending.

Goals
- Address the user's report that Flap Dash feels slow and lethargic.
- Make the hop quicker and the course about 15 percent faster.

Why the tuning works
- Under ideal constant gravity, time to the apex is initial upward speed divided by gravity. The chosen pair reaches the apex about 14 percent sooner while changing theoretical hop height by about 2 percent.
- Increasing only flap strength would make hops higher and could require changing the course. Stronger gravity keeps the motion short.
- Pipe distance spacing remains nearly unchanged: 3.91 units before, about 3.922 units now. The same distance passes more quickly.
- The shorter flap pulse and quicker tilt reduce the duration of decorative motion. Input still enters in Update and physics applies in FixedUpdate.
- Serialized prefab values override script defaults, so both must be updated deliberately.

Debugging lesson
- The first run passed all gameplay checks but its log contained InputManager.get_settings assertions while leaving Play mode.
- Reading Input System's settings getter showed that its current settings object was no longer valid during the transition. The validation helper restored settings in Finish, after the transition had begun.
- Restore the original settings in Stop before ExitPlaymode, then destroy the temporary object during Finish. The rerun passed all 17 checks without those assertions.
- First-run evidence is retained as FlapDash_Validation04_FirstRun.log and FlapDash_Smoke04_FirstRun.json. The JSON alone did not reveal the cleanup issue; always inspect the full log.

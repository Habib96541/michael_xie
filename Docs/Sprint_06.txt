Debugging lesson
- The first validation run failed because the sound was not loaded before the first flap.
- Inspecting FlapChirp.wav.meta showed preloadAudioData: 0, despite choosing DecompressOnLoad. These settings serve different purposes.
- Confirmed the current Unity 6.3 AudioImporterSampleSettings.preloadAudioData API and set it explicitly to true. The importer now records preloadAudioData: 1; the rerun passes all 30 checks.
- The first-run report/log are retained as FlapDash_Smoke06_FirstRun.json and FlapDash_Validation06_FirstRun.log.

Explanation
- AudioSource plays the assigned clip; AudioListener receives the scene's audio for output.
- Triggering playback from the actual flap keeps sound synchronized with accepted actions across all input devices. Reading every raw press separately could chirp on rejected/menu input.
- Restarting one source prevents simultaneous copies from increasing loudness during fast tapping. Volume can be adjusted on the Player AudioSource in the Inspector.

Senior note
- A configured clip is not necessarily loaded, and a playing source is not proof of audible speaker output. Verify asset readiness, source/listener wiring and player listening separately.

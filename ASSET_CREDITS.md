# Asset provenance

All game buildings, vehicles, props, roads, hole meshes, palette shader, UI panel graphics, music and sound effects were created specifically for this project through source-controlled procedural recipes in `MeshWorkshop.cs` and `BoroughBuilder.cs`. No third-party environment or vehicle models were used.

Typography uses **Liberation Sans**, distributed in Unity's TextMeshPro essential resources. Its bundled SIL Open Font License is preserved under `Assets/TextMesh Pro/Fonts/`. TextMeshPro and Unity packages retain their supplied licenses. TMP's imported EmojiOne sprites are not used by the game; their bundled attribution remains with those resources.

The game uses an original title, city layout, mesh designs and interface. It contains no Hole Arena proprietary assets, branding or map data.

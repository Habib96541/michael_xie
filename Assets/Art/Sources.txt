- Non-power-of-two scaling disabled, maximum texture size 2048.
- Panel slice borders: left 16, bottom 16, right 16, top 16 pixels.
- Button slice borders: left 16, bottom 20, right 16, top 16 pixels.
- The frame and pointer use simple images with their aspect ratio preserved.
- Decorative images do not receive pointer events.

Sprint 03: Main menu and sound settings
Date: 2026-11-05

Goals
- Open the game on a main menu with Play, Sound Settings, and Quit.
- Match the existing cream, green, and gold interface.
- Return from a completed round without losing the session bank.
- Block gameplay actions while the menu is open.

Changes
- Added a same-scene title screen with an original UI Toolkit egg illustration.
- Added a sound settings page using the existing saved music, effects, and
  mute preferences. Menu and gameplay controls stay synchronized.
- Play prepares a fresh egg and opens the existing game interface.
- Added Main Menu to both collected and cracked result screens. Returning
  resets the egg, fragments, and round while retaining banked points.
- Drop, collect, and restart actions are ignored in the menu. Mute remains
  available with M. Pending gameplay input is cleared during transitions.
- Quit calls Application.Quit in the built game and is disabled in the editor.
- Added responsive landscape and portrait styles with a scrollable menu body
  for smaller windows. Existing round rules, crack effects, and sounds remain.
- No scene migration or new serialized references are required.
- Follow-up: portrait title and subtitle now have a definite full width, and
  story labels retain their natural height. The portrait story no longer
  grows or shrinks vertically, and scroll content cannot compress it.

Done condition
- The main menu is the first visible screen on entering Play mode.
- Play opens a ready first-floor round without dropping the egg.
- Menu controls open and close sound settings, synchronize sliders and mute,
  and retain preferences across scene reloads.
- Gameplay input cannot operate the egg behind the menu.
- Returning after a completed round restores the egg and preserves the bank.
- Menu actions are visible and usable in landscape and portrait.
- Existing rule, mesh, game-loop, and fracture checks pass.

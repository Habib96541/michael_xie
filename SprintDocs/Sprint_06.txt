How to use
1. Upload the ZIP contents to a static web host, keeping index.html at the root.
2. Serve the folder over HTTP or HTTPS and open index.html in a browser.
3. Serve .wasm files as application/wasm. No compression headers are required.
4. To rebuild in Unity, choose Watermelon > Build WebGL.
5. Output files are written to Builds/WebGL. Recreate the ZIP after rebuilding.

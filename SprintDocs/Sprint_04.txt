Sprint 04: Band and burst sound effects
Date: 2026-11-02

Goals
- Play a short rubber twang for every accepted band addition.
- Play a heavier pop and splash when the watermelon bursts.
- Provide separate volume controls and stop lingering sounds on restart.

Changes
- Created two original synthesized WAV clips with no external audio assets.
- BandTwang.wav is a 0.19-second rubber pluck with a short snap.
- WatermelonBurst.wav is a 0.65-second low pop with noise and wet drop tones.
- Added WatermelonAudio to manage playback through a cached AudioSource.
- Added serialized Band Volume and Burst Volume controls.
- Band audio is triggered after the cooldown guard accepts an addition.
- The burst sound is triggered once alongside the burst visual.
- The final band plays its band sound as well as the burst sound.
- Restart calls AudioSource.Stop to clear ongoing sounds.
- The AudioSource uses 2D playback, with looping and Play On Awake disabled.
- Connected both clips on Round Controller in the saved playable scene.
- Updated the scene builder so recreated scenes include sound effects.
- Added Watermelon > Set Up Sound Effects to configure an existing scene.

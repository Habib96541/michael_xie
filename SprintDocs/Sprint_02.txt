Sprint 02: Shell fracture and egg spill
Date: 2026-11-03

Goals
- Replace the two smooth shell halves with ten irregular curved shell pieces.
- Show shell thickness, physical scattering, and an egg-white and yolk spill.
- Preserve the existing impact sound timing and round rules.
- Restore all fragments and spill objects on every restart.

Changes
- Added EggFracture to manage pre-created shell fragments and spill animation.
- EggBody now records the landing contact point and starts the effect there.
- Fragment velocities are applied in FixedUpdate. Fragments use convex mesh
  colliders, friction, and damping to settle on the landing pad.
- Fragment-to-fragment collision is disabled to prevent intersecting starting
  pieces from producing an explosive launch.
- Added original closed shell meshes with outer, inner, and broken-edge faces.
- Added simplified convex collision meshes sampled from the visible shell
  surfaces, with a small contact margin, and original wet spill materials.
- Added a scene upgrade command and integrated fracture into new scene setup.
- Reused the existing crack sound, which fires in the same landing callback.
- No changes to crack probability, points, collection, or keyboard bindings.
